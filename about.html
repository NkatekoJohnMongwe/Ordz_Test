About Ordz

The Wizard of Ordz...

Who we are

Ordz is a South African hip-hop imprint representing the authentic original artistic elements of hip-hop urban culture.

We are a publisher, a community hub and a discovery engine in one. We connect artists, fans, producers, creatives and industry players, and we put independent South African hip-hop in front of the people who care about it.

Ordz SA is for the authentic hip-hop head.

What we stand for

Authenticity

We back what is real: real voices, real stories, real scenes.

Originality

Original artistic elements first. No copy-paste culture.

Community

Artists, fans, producers and creatives, in one room.

Discovery

Finding the next voice before the algorithm does.

Independence

Our structure is built outside gatekeepers.

Culture

Hip-hop is a way of life as much as a genre.

Expression

Every language, every region, every style has a place.

Knowledge

We document, explain and archive what the culture knows.

Why “Ordz”?

The name comes from the language of hip-hop itself.

  • Words
  • Lyrics
  • Bars
  • Wordplay
  • Language
  • Storytelling

Words are how MCs build worlds, settle battles, tell histories and carry people through hard days. Ordz is a nod to all of it.

In hip-hop, words are currency.
